About

Engineer first, perpetual student second.

I started out as a .NET developer in Gurugram, moved to Germany, and spent a year and a half at trivago building and maintaining the administration platform that advertisers and accommodations run on — React and TypeScript on top of a Node and GraphQL layer, with Vitest coverage on everything I shipped and SOX-compliant review on everything I touched.

Today I am a scientific assistant at RWTH Aachen's process-mining group, working on ToTEM, a Temporal Object-Type Miner for object-centric event logs. I implemented its Object-Centric Causal Nets: you point the tool at an OCEL and get back an interactive, auto-laid-out net you can actually explore.

The thread running through all of it is wanting to understand the layer below the one I am working on. That is what pulled me into data science, into AWS architecture, and into security — where the fastest way to learn how a system really behaves is to try to take it apart.

Outside work I write small, self-contained tools for problems I personally have. Two of them, Lamplight and RoadReady, started exactly that way.

Flagship

RoadReady

roadready.online · TypeScript

A free, browser-based course for the German driving theory test. Most apps just drill the official question catalogue at you — RoadReady teaches the rule first, then tests you on it, following the 14-module curriculum German driving schools are legally required to teach.

Every traffic sign is drawn in code so it stays crisp at any size, and driving situations are generated from data rather than described in a paragraph. The whole interface speaks all 13 languages the official exam is offered in — Arabic included, right-to-left and all — because it was built for people learning to drive in a second language.

German is complete, down to the 30-question mock exam with the official error-point weighting. France is next.

Lamplight

lamplight.ink · single-file HTML

A one-page PDF reader that redraws papers in calm, low-glare colours. Reading white-background PDFs is a small floodlight pointed at your face; Lamplight re-renders every page in a quieter palette, with hue-preserving dark mode so a blue bar in a chart stays blue.

Open a file or paste an arXiv link, keep a shelf of papers with your place in each, and come back tomorrow to find it waiting. Zero dependencies, zero build step, nothing uploaded anywhere — the whole app is one index.html.

Car Price Prediction

Google Cloud App Engine · Python

A resale-price predictor for the Indian used-car market. Enter city, year of purchase, kilometres driven, mileage, engine and power, and it estimates what the car is worth now.

The interesting half was upstream of the model: data cleaning, feature engineering and selection, encoding categorical variables and handling missing data. A linear regression model is trained, serialised with pickle, and served from Flask on GCP App Engine.
